# Travala Homepage Behaviors

## Header
- **Initial:** transparent, overlays hero image
- **After scroll > 10px:** solid `#1e2e5e` background with shadow
- **Mobile menu open:** solid background

## Search widget
- Tab click switches Stays / Flights / Car Rental / Activities
- Autocomplete dropdown on typing (Travala suggestion API)
- Date fields visible for Stays and Car Rental

## Destinations
- Continent thumbnail click updates three carousels (countries, cities, hotels)

## Carousels
- Horizontal scroll with snap on mobile

## FAQ
- Accordion: one item open at a time (toggle)
