# Footer Specification

## Overview
- **Target file:** `src/components/Footer.tsx`

## Structure
- Dark navy background `#1e2e5e`
- Multi-column link groups: Company, Support, Products, Legal
- Social icons row

## Brand Colors
- Links: white/70 hover white
- Accent CTA: `#2dd4bf` (teal)
- Logo: white variant on dark bg

## Responsive
